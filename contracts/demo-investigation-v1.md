# Demo investigation contract, version 1

Status: M5, revisions 395 and 396 (§14 M5's exit gate); the window's entry to it since 396
Owner: `InterCat.Application` (`DemoInvestigation`)
Produced by: `icat demo <directory> [--json]`, and the window's Explore the demo
Read by: every reader of a session or an investigation, which says a demo session was generated

M5's exit gate ships a reproducible demo investigation showing multiple transports and two hosts, with explicit unsupported
rows. InterCat generates it: nothing in it was captured, and no value in it is evidence about Windows. It is the
investigation of §8 over two sessions, so every view of one - the window, its investigation window, and every `icat`
command - can be tried with no capture, no elevation and no second machine.

## 1. What it holds

| Path | What it is |
|---|---|
| `demo.icat-workspace` | The investigation (`workspace-v16`): both sessions, their hosts named `demo client` and `demo server`, the server aligned to the client by their recorded wall clocks with a stated 2 ms synchronization, and a note that opens with the disclosure of §3 |
| `demo-client/` | The client's session, 10.0.0.10: `browser.exe` (PID 4100) and `sync.exe` (PID 4200, which exits at 58 s) send TCP to the server's 443 and 8443, and the browser sends UDP to a name server at 10.0.0.53 that neither capture recorded |
| `demo-server/` | The server's session, 10.0.0.20: `api.exe` (5100) answers 443 and asks `db.exe` (5200) over loopback TCP, and `files.exe` (5300) answers 8443. Its capture began 0.75 s after the client's by the wall clock, and its source session lost 2 records |

Each session records one minute with a 10 MHz clock and is published whole: its journal, segments, coverage ledger
(`coverage-v2`), clock calibration (`clock-calibration-v1`), finalization marker and derivation checkpoint, so it opens
as a finished capture does. Its ledger collects process lifecycle, TCP and UDP and nothing else, so every other
mechanism - named and anonymous pipes, shared sections, RPC and ALPC among them - is listed as not collected: those are
the demo's unsupported rows. The server's loss makes its collected mechanisms read as having a partial gap.

Between the hosts, each TCP connection's two ends are one-sided in their own sessions and mirror each other, so the
investigation finds two candidate joins (443 and 8443), overlapping in its time; the UDP to the unrecorded host has none.
On the server, `api.exe` and `db.exe` are one paired loopback relationship.

## 2. Reproducible

Its records, times and identities are fixed: the client began recording at 2026-01-05 09:00:00 UTC, every session,
capture, clock, host and boot identity is derived from the demo's name for it, and its generations are committed at fixed
times. Two demos made anywhere hold the same sessions, to the digest of every file their generations name. Each
investigation file is one of its own, with an identity of its own, over the same sessions aligned the same way.

It is written only into a new or empty folder. Its investigation file is made under another name and takes its own
last, so a folder that holds `demo.icat-workspace` holds the whole demo, and one that a stopped write left holds none.

## 3. Saying it was generated

Every demo session is published under the source identity `intercat-demo-v1`, and its records name the provider
`InterCat-Demo`, whose identity is derived from that name, so no demo record reads as a Windows provider's. A reader
that finds that source identity says so, in these words: "InterCat generated this demo: nothing in it was captured, and
no value in it is evidence about Windows."

| Reader | Where it says it |
|---|---|
| The window | Its health strip ("Demo session, generated by InterCat"), its title ("Demo session · generation N"), the disclosure beneath its views, and the saved sessions list |
| The investigation window | First among its caveats, when any member is a demo session |
| `icat demo` | Before it writes, and with what it wrote |
| The window's Explore the demo | Its capture card, once the demo is open; the action's description says nothing in it was captured |
| `icat session` | Among its notes |
| `icat workspace show` | After its members, and as each member's `demo` in `workspace-resolution-v19` |

## 4. The window's demo

The window offers Explore the demo beside its saved sessions and investigations, before any capture and whenever none
runs. It keeps the demo in a folder of InterCat's own, `InterCat\Demo` in the user's local application data, beside
`InterCat\Sessions` and never in it, so the saved sessions list holds only what a person saved. The first time, it
writes the demo into `Demo\demo`; afterwards it opens the demo found there as it is, without writing it again. A `demo`
folder that holds anything else is left as it is, and the first of `demo-2` to `demo-100` that holds a demo or nothing
takes its place; when none does, the window says so and names the folder that holds them. It opens the demo's
investigation in the investigation window and says, where the capture card states what happened, what the demo is, in
the words of §3.

## 5. Not defined at this version

- A demo of RPC, ALPC, pipes or content. Their records need more than a transfer's fields; until a demo carries them,
  those mechanisms are among its rows not collected.
- A redacted package of a demo session. It says it is a redacted package (`redacted-session-v1`), whose records are
  synthetic, but not that its source was generated too: its source identity is the package's. An original evidence
  package and an investigation package copy a demo session as it is, and say what it is.
